BalanceU

Your balance, one day at a time.

A student well-being app to organize academic responsibilities and protect time for rest, exercise, meals and connection.

THE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING

University students managing competing academic and personal commitments need realistic ways to protect time for rest and well-being, because fixed schedules, travel and work demands reduce flexibility and recovery time.

From our interviews to the app

Deadlines and time pressure→ Daily task planner
Irregular sleep and recovery→ Rest and check-ins
Stress and academic pressure→ Mood and stress reflection
Exercise and meals overlooked→ Movement and meal routines
